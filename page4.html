The Train

The train finally arrives.

It slowly pulls into the station and comes to a stop directly in front of you.

The doors open.

Nobody gets off.

You look inside. Every seat is empty.

The lights inside the train flicker for a moment.

You look back toward the stranger, but they are gone.

You step onto the train.

The doors close behind you.

Your phone suddenly loses service.

You look out the window as the train begins moving.

The station disappears behind you.

You have never seen this part of the city before.

The train continues into the darkness.
