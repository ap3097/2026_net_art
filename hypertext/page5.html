The End

When you finally step off the train, everything looks familiar.

Your street.

Your house.

Your front door.

You walk toward your house and take out your phone.

The screen lights up.

11:47 PM.

You stare at the time.

Exactly the same time it was when you left the station.

You look back toward the tracks.

The train is already gone.

There is no sound of it anywhere.

You turn toward your house.

Before opening the door, you hear footsteps behind you.

You slowly turn around.

Nobody is there.

The End.
