The Walk Home

You decide not to wait.

You climb the stairs and step out of the station into the cold night air.

The streets are empty. Every store is closed.

Behind you, deep underground, you hear a train pull into the station.

You don't look back.

You walk faster.

Block after block, the streetlights flicker as you pass beneath them.

Finally, you turn the corner.

Your street.

Your house.

Your front door.

You take out your phone.

The screen lights up.

11:47 PM.

You stare at the time.

Exactly the same time it was when you were standing on the platform.

Before opening the door, you hear footsteps behind you.

You slowly turn around.

Nobody is there.

The End.
