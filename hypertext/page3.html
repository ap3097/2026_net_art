The Stranger

You slowly walk toward the person.

As you get closer, you realize they are wearing an old black coat. Their face is difficult to see underneath the station lights.

"Are you waiting for the train too?" you ask.

They don't answer immediately.

Instead, they point toward the tracks.

"That train isn't going where you think it is," they finally say.

You look down the tracks. A small light is getting closer.

You can hear the sound of metal moving against metal.

The stranger looks directly at you.

"Don't get on."

Do you believe them?

Trust the stranger and leave

Ignore the warning and wait for the train
